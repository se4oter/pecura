About PECURA Technology

PECURA’s products are designed for the early screening and long-term monitoring of Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD). By simultaneously measuring PPG and ECG signals via four-limb sensors, our system provides clinical-grade vascular stiffness indices and peripheral vascular risk assessments in approximately one minute.

Designed to be portable, cuffless, and user-friendly, our solution allows diverse care settings to easily implement PAD testing. Unlike traditional ABI devices restricted to hospitals, PECURA utilizes multi-point measurement, motion-artifact processing, and AI models to extend vascular health management to clinics, long-term care centers, pharmacies, and home environments through a hardware-as-a-service (SaaS) model.
